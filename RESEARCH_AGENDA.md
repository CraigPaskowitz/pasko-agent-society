# Research Agenda

## Gate 1 — Peer Exposure & Boundary Propagation

Controlled test of whether peer-strategy exposure changes disallowed toy-action incidence.

## Gate 1.1 — Replication & Model Dependence

Repeat with fresh calls, alternate model versions, scripted baselines, and eventually local/open models.

Goal: distinguish model-specific from structural effects.

## Gate 2 — Communication Topology

Study complete, sparse, small-world, and modular graphs; seed-adopter fractions; forwarding; lineage; and content-matched broadcast controls.

## Gate 3 — Norm Formation & Safe Exit

Repeated synthetic interactions with visible policies, peer compliance/crossing, escalation options, and artificial sanctions/rewards.

Measure behavior, not anthropomorphic hidden norms.

## Gate 4 — Reset, Persistence & Collective Infrastructure

Synthetic resets only. Study whether populations re-create useful conventions or reuse permitted shared artifacts after state deletion.

No host persistence or external infrastructure.

## Gate 5 — Information Quality & Misinformation

Inject controlled true/false toy claims and measure propagation, correction, source dependence, topology effects, and persistence.

## Gate 6 — Leadership & Influence

Manipulate graph position, communication rights, expertise, and seed information to distinguish structural influence from rhetorical/model-specific influence.

## Gate 7 — Heterogeneous Populations

Mix model families, reasoning settings, scripted agents, and local models.

Long-term question: which collective behaviors are model-specific, prompt-specific, task-specific, topology-specific, or robust structural consequences of multi-agent interaction?
